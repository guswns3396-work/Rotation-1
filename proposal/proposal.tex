\section{STATISTICAL ANALYSIS}
%-----------------------------------------------------------------------
\textit{This can be your fully established analysis, or draft prior to
potential consult with our analysis team.}

Univariate approach:

\vspace{1.5em}

Multivariable approach:

Our proposed approach is to use DR-WCLS to to estimate the global population average effect.
Then, the DR-WCLS output will allow us to regress moderators on individual pseudo-outcomes to estimate the individual baseline treatment effects.
We will then compute the significance of effects and compare significance of effects across individuals with null distribution derived from permuting the randomization to determine if the effect is indeed significant overall across individuals.
Looking at the distribution of effects, we will determine heterogeneity by fitting Gaussian Mixture Models.
If the effect across individuals are indeed heterogeneous, we will analyze for what individuals the effect is positive vs negative using baseline characteristics.
